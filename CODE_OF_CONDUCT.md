# Code of conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free project for everyone. Be constructive, assume good intent while evaluating technical claims on evidence, respect privacy, and make room for people with different backgrounds and experience levels.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, sustained disruption, publishing another person's private information, or using project access to retaliate against a contributor.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, release channels, and private project communication. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close or lock threads, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

For a confidential conduct report, use the repository's private reporting channel at <https://github.com/ruby1304/dsh-fleet/security/advisories/new> and state that it is a conduct matter. Do not publish sensitive evidence in an issue. Reports will be reviewed promptly, impartially, and with attention to the safety and privacy of everyone involved.
